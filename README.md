# Comprehensive Master Operating Budget & 3-Statement Financial Model

An end-to-end operational budgeting and financial projection model built for a manufacturing firm (**DTN Boat Company**), integrating operating schedules, dynamic cash flow management, and pro-forma 3-statement financial models.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project simulates the annual financial planning and operational cycle of a manufacturing company across 4 quarters. The objective is to evaluate operational feasibility, forecast working capital requirements, model liquidity constraints, and project full financial statements under specific managerial and financing policies.

---

## 🏗️ Model Architecture & Core Schedules

1. **Sales & Collection Budget:** Forecasted quarterly sales volume ($1.0M annual revenue) and modeled cash receipts using an 80/20 collection policy.
2. **Production & Inventory Planning:** Calculated quarterly production requirements (105 total units) dynamically linked to ending inventory targets (50% of next quarter's demand).
3. **Direct Costs Modeling:** 
   - Direct Materials (Wood & Fiberglass) purchasing schedules with lag payment structures (75% current quarter, 25% subsequent quarter).
   - Direct Labor budget based on standard direct labor hours (80 hours/boat @ $20/hr).
4. **Manufacturing Overhead (MOH) & Product Costing:**
   - Separated variable MOH drivers ($7.5/DLH) and quarterly fixed MOH allocations ($42,000 total).
   - Standardized unit production cost sheet ($3,800/boat).
5. **Selling & Administrative (S&A) Expenses:** Forecasted variable delivery/sales commissions alongside fixed administrative expenditures.
6. **Dynamic Cash Flow & Financing Schedule:**
   - Modeled quarterly cash inflows and disbursements.
   - Incorporated a dynamic short-term credit facility ($4,875 borrowed in Q1, fully repaid with 10% annual interest in Q2) to maintain a mandatory minimum cash reserve of $100,000.
7. **Pro-Forma 3-Statement Integration:**
   - **Budgeted Income Statement:** Net Income after tax projected at $213,826.8.
   - **Budgeted Balance Sheet:** Balanced asset and liability positions at $2,093,878 with CapEx replacement modeling ($20,000 reinvestment).
   - **Statement of Cash Flows (Indirect Method):** Reconciled operational cash flow adjustments, working capital fluctuations, and financing activities.
